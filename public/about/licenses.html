Chess Sathi uses free and open-source software. This page lists what it uses and where to get the source code.

Stockfish (chess engine)

The Game doctor, the Practice tab, puzzle checks and Sathi use Stockfish.js 18 (lite, single-threaded), which runs in your browser. It is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 (GPLv3).

  • Based on Stockfish © T. Romstad, M. Costalba, J. Kiiski, G. Linscott and other contributors: github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
  • Full licence text: GPLv3 (COPYING.txt) · gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0
  • The engine files we serve are unmodified copies of the official release: stockfish-18-lite-single.js and stockfish-18-lite-single.wasm. The source code for exactly this version is in the two repositories above (release “18”).

Games you paste into the Game doctor are checked on your own device. When the check is finished, the result (the mistakes found, not the whole game) is saved to your Chess Sathi account, and only your parent, your teacher and you can see it.
